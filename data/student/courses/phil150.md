# PHIL 150 Philosophy of Technology

## PHIL 150 — Course overview and instructor

PHIL 150 Philosophy of Technology, Fall 2026. Instructor: Dr. Sarah Lindqvist (slindqvist@example.edu). Office hours Thursdays 10:00–12:00 in Humanities Building 118. Seminar Tue/Thu 15:30–16:45. Participation in seminar discussion counts toward the grade.

## PHIL 150 — Grading breakdown

Essay 1 25%, Essay 2 30%, weekly reading responses 20%, seminar participation 15%, final reflection 10%. Essays are submitted through the course site as PDF.

## PHIL 150 — Essay 1 prompt and requirements

Essay 1 is due Friday, October 16, 2026, 11:59 pm. 1,500 words (±10%). Prompt: "Heidegger argues that the essence of technology is nothing technological. Using 'The Question Concerning Technology', explain what he means by *Gestell* (enframing), then apply the idea to one technology you use every day." Requirements: a clear thesis in the first paragraph, at least three direct citations from the Heidegger text, and one counterargument considered seriously. Chicago-style footnotes.

## PHIL 150 — Essay 2 prompt

Essay 2 is due Friday, November 20, 2026. 2,000 words. Prompt: "Does social media extend or erode the self? Answer using Simone de Beauvoir's account of ambiguity and at least one other author from the second half of the syllabus."
