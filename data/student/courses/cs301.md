## CS 301 — Late policy and extensions

Each student has 2 free late days for homework (not the project). After that, late work loses 20% per day. Extension requests beyond late days must be emailed to Prof. Chen at least 48 hours before the deadline, with a brief reason and a proposed new date. Medical or family emergencies are handled through the Dean of Students office and do not need the 48-hour notice.

## CS 301 — Course project description

Teams of 2–3 design and implement a database for a real-world domain (e.g. campus clubs, a food bank, a game leaderboard). Deliverables: ER diagram, normalized schema (3NF), 15+ meaningful queries, indexes with an EXPLAIN-based justification, and a 10-minute demo in the last week of class. Jordan's team: Jordan Reyes, Priya Nair, Sam Okoro. Topic: campus bike-share tracking.
