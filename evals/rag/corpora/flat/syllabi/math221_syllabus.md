MATH 221 Linear Algebra, Fall 2026, 4 credits, Prof. David Okafor. Prerequisite: MATH 120 Calculus II (or instructor permission). Covers systems of linear equations, vector spaces, orthogonality, determinants, eigenvalues and the SVD, with a focus on both computation and proof.
Learning objectives: students will be able to (1) solve linear systems by elimination and describe solution sets, (2) find bases and dimensions of the four fundamental subspaces, (3) compute projections and least-squares solutions, (4) compute and use determinants and eigenvalues, and (5) write short, rigorous proofs about subspaces and linear independence.

Required textbook: Gilbert Strang, *Introduction to Linear Algebra*, 6th edition (Wellesley-Cambridge Press). Problem sets are assigned from it. Strang's MIT OpenCourseWare 18.06 video lectures are recommended as a supplement. No calculators are allowed on quizzes or exams, so practice row reduction by hand.
